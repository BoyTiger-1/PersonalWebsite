    Climate and natural-disaster intelligence, built after wildfires hit my community.

    A full-stack decision-support system that pulls live data from NASA, NOAA, USGS and Open-Meteo and assesses fifteen hazards, from wildfires and floods to earthquakes and air quality.     02 / Research & writing
    Publication · Topological data analysis

    Topological Data Analysis of NFL Defensive Formations: Does Persistent Homology Predict Coverage Breakdowns Beyond Standard Geometric Baselines?

    Eleven defenders are a point cloud. I treat each formation that way and ask whether persistent homology, which tracks the connected pieces and holes that appear as you grow a radius around every point, picks up coverage breakdowns that standard geometric baselines miss.
